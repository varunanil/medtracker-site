Delete your Med Tracker account

Med Tracker is a medicine reminder app for Android, made by Varun Anil. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open Med Tracker and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete account again.

The phone needs to be online. The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email medtracker@ap1.varunanil.dev from the email address you sign in with (or your Google account's email, if you sign in with Google), asking for your account to be deleted.

We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. If the request comes from a different address, we will ask you to confirm from the account's address first.

What is deleted

Everything Med Tracker stores for your account on its server:

  • your account and sign-in details
  • your medicines, schedules, refills and dose history
  • your settings, your list of phones, and the tokens used to send you alerts
  • your sharing: the people who see your medicines, the people whose medicines you see, and the share links you made
  • missed-dose alerts sent to or about you

People you shared with stop seeing your medicines at once. They are not notified.

What is kept

  • A record that an account was deleted, with the date and how many medicines, doses, phones and sharing connections it had. It holds no name, email or account id.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps a log of sign-in events, and daily backups for 7 days. Your data leaves the backups after 7 days.

On your phone

Deleting the account does not remove what is on the phone you used as your main phone: your medicines and reminders stay there, so reminders keep ringing. Uninstall the app to remove them. On your other phones, the account's medicines are removed.

Starting again

You can sign in again any time with the same email or Google account. That makes a new, empty account.
